Overview

Negril is a relaxed resort town on the western tip of Jamaica, known for its beaches, sunsets and cliffs.

Attractions

Things to see and do

  • Seven Mile Beach
  • Negril Lighthouse
  • The West End cliffs
  • Royal Palm Reserve

7 Cures for a Lean Purse

The Seven Cures for a Lean Purse taught me to save consistently, control my spending, grow and protect my money, make wise housing decisions, prepare for the future, and improve my ability to earn. The cure I can apply to my life is the second cure: controlling my spending. I sometimes prioritise my wants over my needs, which can cost me more in the long run. Recently, I have started thinking carefully before buying something. If it is not a need, I choose not to purchase it.

5 Laws of Gold

The Five Laws of Gold taught me to save regularly, invest wisely, seek knowledgeable advice, understand where my money goes, and avoid promises of quick riches. The law I can apply to my life is the first law: saving at least 10% of what I earn. Since I am becoming more aware of my needs and wants, I can save some of the money I would normally spend on unnecessary purchases. This will help me prepare for my future.

My Biggest Takeaway

My biggest takeaway is that managing money starts with my everyday choices. By putting my needs first and saving regularly, I can build better financial habits and prepare for my future.
